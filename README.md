## How fast should your internet be?

This is one of those things that sounds obvious until a stream starts buffering at the exact moment the interesting bit happens.

TiviLive's current installation guide lists **15 Mbps+** as a basic connection target and **25 Mbps+ recommended for 4K/UHD**, while also preferring a wired Ethernet connection for stability. Its FAQ similarly recommends around 25 Mbps or more for 4K content.

For a Windows setup, a practical approach is:

* HD viewing: a stable broadband connection should be enough.
* 4K: aim for at least 25 Mbps of reliable bandwidth.
* If possible, use Ethernet when Wi-Fi is inconsistent.
* If one channel buffers while others play normally, the issue may be specific to that stream rather than the entire PC setup.

In other words, before blaming Windows, check the network. Computers are surprisingly good at taking responsibility for problems they did not cause.
